借一篇英语周记，记录我最近折腾的AI

九月 13, 2026 by DmDh

其实，这篇周记是新学期的一项英语作业。

说是周记，其实也没什么特别的要求。写写这一周发生了什么，最近在做些什么，或者只是记录一点自己的想法，都可以。

我想了想，这周倒也没有什么特别值得一提的大事。不过，如果说最近我一直在折腾些什么，那应该是 AI 了。这段时间，我又重拾了部分编程本事，也开始试着用 AI 做一些诸如网站界面、工具箱一类的小东西。

所以，这次的英语周记，我决定就写这个。

My Week with AI

This week, I spent some of my free time playing with AI. As you know, I really like trying interesting things. So, I recently tried something new called “vibe coding”.

You may have a question: What is "vibe coding"?

It is a way of using AI to help with coding. Instead of writing everything by myself, I can just tell AI what I want to make, and it helps me create it. I tried doing this with DeepSeek, and I found it really interesting.

I used it to make some small things and improve my own website. Of course, it didn’t always work perfectly. Sometimes I had to change my ideas or ask it to fix some problems. But I actually enjoyed this process.

A few weeks ago, I created my own website, “dmdh.me”. It's a personal blog site. I use it to write something I like, record some of my ideas, and share things I've made. Recently, I also started thinking about how to make it better with the help of AI.

I really like the feeling of seeing my ideas become real in a short time. I wasn’t simply asking AI for answers. I was trying things, making mistakes, and finding ways to improve them. Nowadays, AI has almost become my agent, my copilot and my good teacher.

I’m still learning about programming, so there are many things I don’t understand yet. But I think it's OK. I’m curious, and I like learning things by trying them myself.

That’s probably what I enjoyed most this week. I learned something new, made something interesting, and had a lot of fun.

写到这里，这篇作为英语作业的周记，也就成了博客里的一篇小文章。

就这样把最近的我记录下来吧。
